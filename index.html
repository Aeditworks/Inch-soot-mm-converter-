How to Use
Usage Guide
1
Sirf inch: 27 ya 100 — seedha likho, result aa jaayega.
2
Inch + Soot: 27"4 likho — yahi is app ka asli fayda hai. Quote ke baad soot number (0 se 7 tak). 27"4 = 27 inch 4 soot = 699 mm.
3
27.5 bhi kaam karta hai — aur same result deta hai jaise 27"4 (dono = 699 mm). Lekin agar haath mein 27 inch 4 soot hai toh seedha 27"4 hi likho — decimal mein convert karne ki zaroorat nahi. More examples: 22"6 = 578 mm, 36"0 = 914 mm, 12"3 = 308 mm.
4
MM se inch/soot: Doosre box mein MM daalo jaise 699 — result 27"4 format mein aayega.

About

Who is this for?

Anyone who works with measurements on-site — carpenters, fabricators, civil engineers, contractors. In India, soot (1/8 inch) is standard on drawings, but calculators don't understand that format. This tool does.

Why does MM round to nearest whole number?

Because 0.3–0.5 mm doesn't matter in real cutting. 988.7 mm showing as 989 mm is cleaner and just as accurate for field use.

Works offline too

Open once, it caches in your browser. No internet needed after that — add to home screen for quick access.
